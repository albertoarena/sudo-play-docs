Sudo Play

A small macOS menu bar app that picks today's Spotify playlist, based on what you are coding and how you feel about it.

Download for Mac (opens in a new tab)

1.0.2 for Apple silicon (M1 or later), 148 MB. Signed and notarized.

Install has the steps for opening Sudo Play the first time.

Pick a genre, like rock or Lo-fi. Pick a developer mood, like Friday Deploy or Debugging. Press Play, and the playlist opens in Spotify.

The pick is the same all day, and the same for everyone who chooses the same genre and mood. Tomorrow it changes.

The line at the top of this page is the tagline, not a command. There is nothing to type in a terminal.

Your data

The app has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The app downloads one catalog file and opens public playlist links. Nothing about you leaves your Mac.
