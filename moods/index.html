Moods

A mood is how you feel about what you are coding. You choose a genre and a mood, and the app picks one playlist for today.

The moods and genres come from the app's catalog. The genres are Classical, Rock, Pop, Indie, Electronic, Jazz and Lo-fi.

MoodFeels like
🎉 Shipped ItHappy, something just went live.
☕ Coffee NeededTired, slow start.
😔 Blue MondayLow energy, start of the week.
🎧 Flow StateDeep focus, no distractions.
🐛 DebuggingCalm and patient, a long investigation.
🚀 Friday DeployBrave, energetic, a bit risky.
🤖 Agent ModeCalm, watching the machines work.
🌙 Night ShiftWired, past midnight, one more commit.

Add a playlist

Each playlist in the catalog has one genre and one or more moods. Anyone can add a playlist with a pull request. The app's README explains how, in Adding a playlist (opens in a new tab).
